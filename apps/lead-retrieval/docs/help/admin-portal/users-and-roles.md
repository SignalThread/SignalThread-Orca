# Users and Roles

Users and roles control what each person can do in the Admin Portal and mobile app. Access is based on the person's role plus the company and event access assigned to them.

---

## Roles at a Glance

**Platform Admin**
Platform Admins support the full SignalThread workspace. They can manage companies, events, licenses, users, and operational settings across customers where platform access is intended.

**Organizer Admin**
Organizer Admins manage event operations for events they are responsible for. Depending on the event setup, they may manage exhibitors, event users, and event-level activity.

**Exhibitor Admin**
Exhibitor Admins manage their company's event workspace. They can usually view and manage company leads, team members, Campaign Agents, campaign workflows, imports, and settings for events their company can access.

**Viewer**
Viewers have limited access. They can usually view assigned event information and use allowed mobile workflows, but they should not be able to manage team access or make high-impact Admin changes.

---

## Company and Event Access

Most Admin Portal views are scoped to a company, an event, or both.

- **Company access** means the user belongs to a company workspace.
- **Event access** means the user can work inside one or more event workspaces.
- Some users may have access to all events for their company.
- Other users may be assigned to specific events only.

If a user cannot see an event, lead, Campaign Agent, campaign, or team member, first confirm that they are in the right company and have access to the selected event.

---

## Pending Invites and Active Users

An invited teammate may show as **Pending** until they accept the invite and complete sign-in.

Pending users may not appear in every active-user list yet because they have not finished account activation. After activation, their access should match the role and event access selected during the invite flow.

---

## Why Something May Be Missing

**A user is missing.**
They may belong to another company, be assigned to a different event, still be pending, or have been removed.

**An event is missing.**
The user may not have event access, or the selected company may not be connected to that event.

**A lead is missing.**
The lead may belong to a different event, a different company, or a filter/search may be hiding it.

**A Campaign Agent or campaign is missing.**
The user may not have access to the selected event or company workspace, or the workflow may require an admin role.
