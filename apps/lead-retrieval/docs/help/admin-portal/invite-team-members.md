# Invite Team Members

Invite teammates from the Admin Portal when they need access to your company's event workspace. The invite determines the person's role and the events they can use.

---

## Invite a User

1. Open the Admin Portal.
2. Go to the Users or Team area.
3. Choose **Invite user** or the equivalent team invite action.
4. Enter the person's name and email address.
5. Choose a role.
6. Choose the company and event access.
7. Send the invite.

Use the person's work email whenever possible so their login identity matches the invite.

---

## Choose a Role

Choose the least broad role that still lets the teammate do their job.

- Use **Exhibitor Admin** for teammates who manage leads, users, imports, campaigns, or settings for your company.
- Use **Viewer** for teammates who need limited access to assigned events or mobile workflows.
- Platform and organizer roles are usually assigned by SignalThread support or event operations teams.

---

## Choose Event or Company Access

Some invite flows let you choose specific events. Others may grant access to all events in the company workspace, depending on your license and account setup.

If a teammate needs mobile access for an event, confirm that the event is included before sending the invite.

---

## Pending Invite Status

After an invite is sent, the user may appear as **Pending** until they accept the invite and finish sign-in.

Pending users are not always shown in the same places as active users. If a user has not accepted the invite, resend it or confirm the email address.

---

## Resend, Remove, or Revoke

Use **Resend invite** when the original email was missed or expired.

Use **Remove invite** or **Delete** when the invite was sent to the wrong person or is no longer needed.

For active users, removing access should revoke their access to the relevant company or event workspace. It should not delete historical lead activity.

---

## Common Invite Issues

**The invite went to the wrong email address.**
Remove the pending invite and send a new one to the correct email.

**The user accepted the invite but cannot see the event.**
Confirm the selected event and company access. The user may need an event assignment.

**The user appears pending after accepting.**
Ask the user to sign out and sign back in. If the status does not update, contact support.

**The user does not receive the invite.**
Check spam filtering, confirm the email address, and use resend.
