# Search Leads

Search helps you find the right lead quickly during event follow-up. Use it when you remember a name, company, title, or contact detail and need to jump straight to the record.

---

## Search the List

1. Open **Leads**.
2. Tap the search field.
3. Enter a name, company, email, phone number, or title.
4. Tap a result to open **Lead Detail**.

Results update as you type.

---

## Clear Search

Tap the clear icon in the search field to return to the full list.

If you are reviewing several leads, clear search before switching to a new task so you do not miss recent captures.

---

## Search Tips

- Use company names when you cannot remember the attendee's name.
- Search by domain or partial email when contact details are easier to recall.
- Keep search terms short if you are unsure how a badge or card was parsed.

---

## Common Issues

**A lead does not appear.**
Clear the search and refresh the list. If the lead was captured offline, it may still be waiting to sync.

**The result looks incomplete.**
Open the lead and clean up the contact fields from **Lead Detail**.
