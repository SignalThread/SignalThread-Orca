# Leads List Overview

The Leads list is your event pipeline in motion. Use it to scan lead quality quickly, find the right record, and make lightweight updates without opening every detail page.

---

## What Each Lead Card Shows

Each row gives you the context needed to decide what to do next:

- Lead name and company
- Title or role, when available
- Star rating
- Temperature
- Follow-up date, if set
- Recent status or activity indicators

This makes it easier to triage leads between conversations or during end-of-day review.

---

## Open and Review a Lead

Tap any lead to open **Lead Detail**, where you can edit fields, add notes, review AI context, and manage follow-up.

Pull down on the list to refresh if new captures or updates are still syncing.

---

## Edit from the List

Long-press a lead to open list-level actions. Depending on your access, you can update key fields such as name, title, company, rating, and follow-up date without leaving the list.

Use list edits for quick cleanup. Use **Lead Detail** when you need richer context, recordings, or AI Insights.

---

## Delete from the List

Long-press a lead and select **Delete** when the record should be permanently removed. Review the confirmation carefully; deletion cannot be undone.

See [Delete a Lead](../lead-detail/delete-lead.md).
