# Voice Notes Offline

Voice notes are built for real event conditions. If connection drops, the app saves the recording on your device and uploads it automatically when service returns.

---

## How Offline Notes Work

When you record while offline:

1. The audio saves locally after **Stop & save**.
2. The note appears with **Uploading when connection improves...**.
3. When connection returns, upload resumes in the background.
4. Status progresses through upload, transcription, and transcript readiness.

---

## What Works Offline

| Action | Offline support |
|--------|-----------------|
| Record a new voice note | Yes |
| Play back a note recorded on this device | Yes |
| View an existing transcript | Yes, if already processed |
| Generate new AI Insights | No, processing requires upload |

---

## Multiple Queued Notes

You can record several notes while offline. They queue on the device and upload when connection improves, so your team can keep capturing context without waiting for Wi-Fi.

---

## Check Upload Status

1. Open the lead.
2. Tap **View all notes**.
3. Review each note's status label.

If notes stay queued after reconnecting, confirm the connection is stable and reopen the app. Upload resumes automatically.

---

## Event-Floor Tips

- Use cellular when venue Wi-Fi is congested.
- Keep recordings concise when connection is weak.
- Leave the app open after reconnecting so queued notes can upload.
