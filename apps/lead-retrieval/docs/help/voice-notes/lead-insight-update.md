# How Voice Notes Update AI Insights

AI Insights become more useful as your team adds more context. Each processed voice note gives SignalThread Scan more detail to work with, helping surface clearer needs, objections, sentiment, and next steps.

---

## How Updates Work

1. You record a meeting or context voice note.
2. The audio uploads and is transcribed.
3. Once the transcript is ready, AI Insights can refresh using the full set of available notes.
4. The **Context & Notes** card reflects the latest status.

A short clarification after a meeting can meaningfully improve the quality of the lead summary.

---

## When Insights Refresh

Insights can update after each note is transcribed. If several notes are added close together, you may see processing status change more than once as the lead context catches up.

---

## Check Status

Open the lead and review the **Context & Notes** card on the Overview tab.

- **Processing insights...** means the update is in progress
- **Insights ready** means the latest available context has been applied

Scroll to the AI Insights section to review the output.

---

## Tips for Stronger Insights

- Add notes soon after the conversation.
- Mention specific needs, timelines, budget signals, objections, or stakeholders.
- Correct important transcript misunderstandings with a short clarification note.
