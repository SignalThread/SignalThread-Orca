# Add a Context Voice Note

Context voice notes help you preserve the details that do not always make it into the main conversation recording: budget nuance, timing, objections, internal next steps, or anything you want AI Insights to consider.

---

## When to Add One

Add a context voice note when:

- The conversation just ended and details are fresh
- You need to clarify something from the recording
- You skipped recording but still want a summary
- You remembered an important follow-up detail later

Short, specific notes are often the most useful.

---

## Record a Context Note

1. Open a lead from **Leads**.
2. In the **Overview** tab, tap **+ Add more context**.
3. When the microphone button turns red, speak your note.
4. Tap **Stop & save**.

You can also tap **View all notes** and record from the Voice Notes sheet.

---

## After Saving

The note is attached to the lead and begins uploading. Once transcribed, it can improve the lead's AI Insights by adding more complete context.

See [How Voice Notes Update AI Insights](lead-insight-update.md) and [Voice Note Status Labels](voice-note-status-labels.md).

---

## Multiple Notes

You can add more than one voice note to a lead. Each note contributes to the lead's context as it is processed.

---

## Common Issues

**The recording control does not respond.**
Microphone access may be blocked. See [Camera & Microphone Permissions](../permissions/camera-microphone-permissions.md).

**The note ended too soon.**
Record another short note with the missing detail.

**You are offline.**
The note saves on your device and uploads when connection returns. See [Voice Notes Offline](voice-notes-offline.md).
