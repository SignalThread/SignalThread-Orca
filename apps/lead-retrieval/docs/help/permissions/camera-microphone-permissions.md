# Camera & Microphone Permissions

Camera and microphone access power the core event workflows: scanning leads, capturing business cards, and recording conversation context. Set permissions before the show starts so your team can capture without interruption.

---

## First-Time Setup

The app asks for access the first time a workflow needs it:

- **Camera** - badge scanning and business card capture
- **Microphone** - meeting recordings and context voice notes

Tap **Allow** when prompted. If you are preparing devices before an event, open the capture and recording workflows once to confirm permissions are ready.

---

## If You Chose "Not Now"

You can continue using the app, but scanning and recording will not work until access is granted. Reopen the workflow to trigger the prompt again, or update permissions from device settings.

---

## Re-Enable Access on iPhone

1. Open the iOS **Settings** app.
2. Find **SignalThread Scan**.
3. Turn on **Camera** and **Microphone**.
4. Return to the app and try the workflow again.

---

## Re-Enable Access on Android

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Go to **Apps**.
3. Select **SignalThread Scan**.
4. Open **Permissions**.
5. Allow **Camera** and **Microphone**.

---

## Common Issues

**The camera opens to a blank screen.**
Confirm camera access is enabled, then close and reopen the app.

**Recording does not start.**
Microphone permission may be blocked. Re-enable access in device settings.

**Permissions are enabled but capture still fails.**
Restart the app and confirm no other app is actively using the camera or microphone.
