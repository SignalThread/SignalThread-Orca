# Campaign Agents

Campaign Agents are reusable email-writing agents used by the Admin Portal to help draft better campaign and follow-up emails.

They do not send emails by themselves. They help a campaign draft understand what matters: product interest, budget cues, competitor mentions, buying timeline, meeting asks, tone, and follow-up goals.

---

## What a Campaign Agent Does

A Campaign Agent gives the draft generator reusable guidance. It can shape what the message emphasizes, what context it uses, and what action it should drive.

Use Campaign Agents when your team wants consistent outreach guidance without rewriting the same instructions for every campaign.

---

## How Campaign Agents Support Campaigns

When building a campaign, users can choose one or more Campaign Agents. The selected agents help the generated draft understand:

- Which product interest or audience cue matters
- Whether the message should adapt to lead context
- What background facts or event context to include
- Which buying signals or objections to acknowledge
- What meeting ask, demo request, or follow-up action should close the message

Always review generated campaign content before approving or sending.

---

## Agent Types

**AI-Powered**
Interprets intent and lead context. Use this when the draft should adapt tone, emphasis, or messaging based on what happened with the lead.

**Contextual**
Adds background facts, buying cues, event context, or audience details so campaign drafts are more relevant.

**Custom**
Reusable copy or instructions that should appear consistently in campaign drafts with only light refinement.

**Call-to-Action**
Guides the close. Use this to define the next step, meeting ask, demo request, or follow-up action.

---

## Who Can View Campaign Agents

Campaign Agent visibility depends on role, company access, and event access.

Exhibitor Admins should generally be able to view Campaign Agents available to their company and event workspace. Viewers may have limited or read-only access depending on the workflow.

---

## Who Can Create or Edit Campaign Agents

Campaign Agent creation and editing are usually admin-level actions. If create or edit controls are disabled or missing, your role may not allow Campaign Agent management.

Contact an admin or support if the wrong person appears to own a Campaign Agent or if an agent should be available to another workspace.

---

## Global and Company Agents

Some Campaign Agents may be global defaults available across workspaces. Company-scoped Campaign Agent inheritance should be treated as planned or workspace-dependent unless it is clearly enabled in your Admin Portal.

Planned/future: broader company Campaign Agent inheritance and governance may become available as the Campaign Agents workflow expands.

---

## Common Access Issues

**I cannot see Campaign Agents.**
Confirm your role, selected event, and company access.

**I cannot edit a Campaign Agent.**
You may have read-only access or the Campaign Agent may be managed by another scope.

**Campaign Builder shows no Campaign Agents.**
Confirm Campaign Agents exist for the selected event or company workspace and that your role can use campaign workflows.
