# Campaign Troubleshooting FAQ

Use this FAQ when a campaign, recipient list, Campaign Agent, or follow-up draft does not behave as expected.

---

## I Cannot Access Campaign Agents

Confirm your role and event access. Campaign Agent management is usually an admin-level workflow, and some Campaign Agents may be read-only or workspace-managed.

---

## I Cannot Generate a Campaign

Campaign generation may require selected recipients, at least one Campaign Agent, and enough lead context. If the button is disabled, check the current selections and any warning messages on the page.

Campaign sending requires live send controls, configured email delivery, and the right role. If sending is unavailable, ask an admin whether sending is enabled for your workspace.

---

## My Campaign Recipients Are Empty

Clear filters, confirm the selected event, and make sure the leads you expect are available in that workspace. Also confirm the leads have usable contact details for the campaign workflow.

---

## My Draft Is Too Generic

Review the selected Campaign Agents, recipient list, lead context, and event context. Add more specific guidance or edit the draft manually before approval.

---

## My Event Is Not Showing

Confirm you are in the right company workspace. Some users can access all company events, while others only see assigned events.

If the event still does not appear, ask an admin to review your event access.

---

## Campaign Sending Is Unavailable

Sending is available only when the workspace has live send controls, configured email delivery, recipients with usable contact details, and a role allowed to send.

---

## Campaign Agents Are Not Integrations

Campaign Agents help shape campaign drafts. They do not connect external systems or send emails by themselves.
