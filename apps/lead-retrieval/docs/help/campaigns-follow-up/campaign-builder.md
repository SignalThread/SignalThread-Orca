# Campaign Builder

Campaign Builder helps teams draft follow-up messages from selected leads, Campaign Agents, and event context. It is designed to speed up campaign preparation while keeping humans in control of the final content.

---

## Create a Campaign

1. Open Campaign Builder from the Admin Portal.
2. Choose the event or company workspace if prompted.
3. Select the leads or audience segment.
4. Choose the Campaign Agents that should guide the draft.
5. Generate a campaign draft.
6. Review and edit the content before approval.

---

## Select Recipients

Recipient selection may come from selected leads, filters, or a campaign audience builder depending on your workspace.

If the recipient list is empty, check the selected event, company access, filters, and whether the leads have usable contact details.

---

## Choose Campaign Agents

Campaign Agents guide the campaign draft. Choose agents that match the message goal, such as product interest, timing, fit, or follow-up priority.

If no Campaign Agents appear, confirm your event and company access, and check whether Campaign Agents exist for the workspace.

---

## Generate and Edit a Draft

Generated campaign content should be reviewed before use. Edit the subject, body, call to action, and any personalization details so the message matches your team's voice and event context.

AI-generated content can help with a first pass, but approval should stay with the campaign owner.

---

## Draft, Ready, and Sent States

Campaign state names may vary by workspace, but common states include:

- **Draft** - content is still being prepared.
- **Ready** - content has been reviewed and is ready for the next step.
- **Sent** - the campaign send step has completed for the available recipients.

Campaign sending is available only when your workspace shows live send controls and email delivery is configured. When enabled, the Admin Portal sends the approved subject and body to campaign recipients through the configured email delivery service.

---

## Common Campaign Generation Issues

**Generate is disabled.**
Select at least one recipient and one Campaign Agent if the workflow requires Campaign Agents.

**The draft is too generic.**
Add more relevant Campaign Agents, review lead context, and edit the draft manually.

**Recipients are empty.**
Check filters, selected event, company access, and contact fields.

**Sending is unavailable.**
Your workspace may not have live sending enabled, email delivery may not be configured, or your role may not have permission to send. Use the approved draft according to your team's process or ask an admin to confirm whether sending is enabled.
