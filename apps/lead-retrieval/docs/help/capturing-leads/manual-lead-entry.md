# Manual Lead Entry

Manual entry keeps capture moving when scanning is not the best option. Use it after a conversation, when a badge is unavailable, or when you need to create a lead from partial information quickly.

---

## When Manual Entry Helps

Manual entry is best for leads who should be in your event pipeline even if you do not have a scannable badge or business card. A name, company, email, or phone number is enough to start the record and add context later.

---

## Add a Lead Manually

1. Tap the **Capture** tab.
2. Select **Manual entry**.
3. Enter the details you have:
   - Full name
   - Job title
   - Company
   - Email
   - Phone
4. Tap **Save lead**.

You do not need every field. At least one of name, company, email, or phone is required so the record can be saved.

---

## After Saving

The lead is added to the active event and opens in **Lead Detail**. From there, add ratings, follow-up dates, voice notes, and any missing contact details so the record is ready for team review.

See [After Scanning](after-scanning.md) for the full post-capture workflow.

---

## Common Issues

**"Save lead" is unavailable.**
Enter at least one identifying field: name, company, email, or phone.

**A detail was entered incorrectly.**
Open the lead from the list and edit the field directly. See [Edit Lead Info](../lead-detail/edit-lead-info.md).
