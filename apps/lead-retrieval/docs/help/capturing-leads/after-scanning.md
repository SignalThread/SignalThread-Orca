# After Scanning: What Happens Next

Every captured lead is immediately organized for follow-up. Whether the contact came from a badge scan, business card scan, or manual entry, SignalThread Scan opens the lead record so your team can qualify the conversation while the details are still fresh.

---

## The Lead Record Opens

After capture, the app opens the **Lead Detail** screen with the available contact information already filled in. Use this moment to confirm the basics, add qualification signals, and keep the conversation connected to the right event.

---

## If Auto-Record Is On

When **Auto-record meetings** is enabled, recording starts as soon as the lead opens. This is useful for busy event teams because the conversation is captured without an extra tap.

The recording indicator appears at the bottom of the screen while audio is active. You can stop the recording at any time.

See [Record a Meeting or Conversation](../recording-and-ai/record-meeting-conversation.md) and [Auto-Record Meetings](../settings-and-account/auto-record-meetings.md).

---

## Qualify the Lead While It Is Fresh

**Rate the lead**
Use the star rating to capture overall value or fit.

**Set the temperature**
Mark the lead as **Hot**, **Warm**, or **Cold** so follow-up urgency is clear.

**Record the conversation**
Start or stop recording from the microphone control when you want AI-ready context.

**Clean up contact details**
Edit missing or incorrect fields before the lead moves into follow-up.

---

## After the Conversation

Before moving to the next booth conversation:

1. Stop the recording if one is running.
2. Add a short voice note with details the recording may not have captured.
3. Set a follow-up date if the lead needs action after the event.

---

## AI Processing Starts Automatically

Once audio uploads, SignalThread Scan creates a transcript and prepares AI Insights for the lead. Processing time depends on connection quality and recording length, but the work happens in the background.

The **Context & Notes** card shows progress:

- **Processing transcript...** - the transcript is being created
- **Transcript ready** - the transcript is available; insights may still be updating
- **Insights ready** - AI Insights are available in the Overview tab

See [AI Insights Not Ready](../recording-and-ai/ai-insights-not-ready.md) if results are taking longer than expected.
