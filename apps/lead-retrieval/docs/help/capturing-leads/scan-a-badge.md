# Scan a Badge

Badge scanning is the fastest way to turn event conversations into structured lead records. Point the camera at a barcode or QR code, capture the attendee details, and move straight into qualification.

---

## Before You Start

Make sure camera access is enabled before the event floor gets busy. If access has not been granted, the app prompts you the first time you scan.

See [Camera & Microphone Permissions](../permissions/camera-microphone-permissions.md) for setup help.

---

## Scan the Badge

1. Tap the **Capture** tab.
2. Point the camera at the badge barcode or QR code.
3. Hold steady until the code is read.
4. Review the lead when it opens in **Lead Detail**.

---

## What Happens Next

- Contact details are populated from the badge data.
- The lead is saved to the active event.
- If **Auto-record meetings** is enabled, recording begins so the conversation context is captured.

See [After Scanning](after-scanning.md) for the recommended follow-up flow.

---

## Common Issues

**The barcode is not reading.**
Move closer, keep the code centered, and avoid glare or shadows on the badge.

**The camera did not open.**
Camera permission may be blocked. Re-enable camera access in your device settings, or see [Camera & Microphone Permissions](../permissions/camera-microphone-permissions.md).

**Some fields look incorrect.**
Badge formats vary by event provider. Edit the lead directly from **Lead Detail**. See [Edit Lead Info](../lead-detail/edit-lead-info.md).

**The event does not use scannable badges.**
Use business card capture or manual entry to keep every meaningful conversation in the system.
