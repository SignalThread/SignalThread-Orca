# Scan a Business Card or Badge

Business card capture turns printed contact information into a usable lead record without manual typing. It is ideal for smaller events, meetings away from the booth, or any situation where badge data is not available.

---

## Before You Start

This workflow uses the camera. Confirm camera access before the event or grant it when prompted.

See [Camera & Microphone Permissions](../permissions/camera-microphone-permissions.md).

---

## Capture the Card

1. Tap the **Capture** tab.
2. Select **Scan card/badge**.
3. Position the card or printed badge so the text is clear and fills the frame.
4. Tap **Capture**.
5. Wait a few seconds while the app reads the lead details.

For best results, avoid glare, hold steady, and move close enough for the text to be sharp.

### If Scanning Is Not Available

If scanning is not available, use **Manual Lead Entry** to capture the lead details directly.

---

## Review Extracted Details

Before saving, review the detected fields:

- Full name
- Job title
- Company
- Email
- Phone

You can type over any value or choose from other detected text when the app offers alternatives.

1. Correct any fields that need cleanup.
2. Add optional notes if helpful.
3. Tap **Create lead**.

---

## After Saving

The lead is saved to the active event and opens in **Lead Detail** so you can qualify it, add notes, and set follow-up.

See [After Scanning](after-scanning.md).

---

## Common Issues

**The scan quality warning appears.**
The card may be hard to read because of lighting, glare, or angle. Try scanning again or continue to review and correct the fields manually.

**No text was detected.**
Move closer, improve lighting, and make sure the card is not angled away from the camera.

**A field pulled the wrong text.**
Use the field picker or type the correct value manually before creating the lead.
