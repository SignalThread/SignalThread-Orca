# Lead Detail Overview

Lead Detail is the working record for a single prospect. It brings together contact information, qualification signals, notes, recordings, AI context, and follow-up actions so the next move is clear.

---

## The Lead Card

The card at the top gives you the fastest read on the lead:

- **Name** and **job title** - editable when a scan needs cleanup
- **Company** - the organization associated with the lead
- **Star rating** - your value or fit signal
- **Temperature** - urgency for follow-up

---

## Tabs

When server processing is available, Lead Detail includes:

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **Overview** | Contact details, enrichment, voice notes, AI Insights, and follow-up fields |
| **AI Brief** | Pre-meeting context generated from public and event data |

The **AI Brief** tab remains unavailable until the brief is ready. If tabs do not appear, the lead may still be syncing.

---

## Overview Tab

Use the Overview tab to keep the lead ready for action:

**Editable contact fields**
- Email
- Phone

**Follow-up context**
- Follow-up date
- Created date

**Enrichment**
Additional company or professional context when available, such as seniority, company size, industry, LinkedIn, or website.

**Context & Notes**
Voice note count, recent activity, and processing status. Tap **+ Add more context** to record another note.

**AI Insights**
When ready, insights summarize needs, objections, buying signals, sentiment, and suggested next steps.

---

## AI Brief Tab

The AI Brief helps you prepare for a conversation. It can include:

- A snapshot of the person and company
- Why the lead may be relevant at this event
- Conversation starters and questions
- Competitive or market context
- Cues to watch during the meeting

See [AI Brief](../recording-and-ai/ai-brief.md).

---

## Save Updates

When you edit a field, tap **Save** in the header. The button changes from **Saving...** to **Saved** once the record is updated.
