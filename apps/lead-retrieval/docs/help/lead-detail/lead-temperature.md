# Lead Temperature

Temperature signals follow-up urgency. Mark leads as **Hot**, **Warm**, or **Cold** so the team can prioritize outreach while event momentum is still high.

---

## Set Temperature

1. Open a lead from **Leads** or **Priority**.
2. Tap **Hot**, **Warm**, or **Cold** on the lead card.
3. The selection saves immediately.

You can also update temperature from the Leads list.

---

## What Each Temperature Means

| Temperature | Use it when |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Hot** | The lead is a strong fit, ready for outreach, or needs immediate follow-up |
| **Warm** | The lead is interested but needs nurturing or follow-up after the event |
| **Cold** | The lead is low priority, early stage, or captured mainly for the record |

---

## How Temperature Supports Priority

If temperature has not been set manually, SignalThread Scan can derive it from platform signals:

- Score 80 or above - **Hot**
- Score 50-79 - **Warm**
- Below 50 - **Cold**

Manual changes take precedence, so your team can override automated scoring with real conversation context.
