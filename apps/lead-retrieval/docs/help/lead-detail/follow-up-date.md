# Follow-Up Date

Follow-up dates turn event conversations into accountable next steps. Add one when a lead needs outreach after the show, a scheduled check-in, or a reminder tied to a buying timeline.

---

## Set a Follow-Up Date

1. Open a lead from **Leads**.
2. In the **Overview** tab, find **Follow-up date**.
3. Tap the calendar icon.
4. Select the date.
5. Tap **Save**.

---

## Change or Remove a Date

To change a date, open the picker again, choose a new date, and tap **Save**.

To remove a date, clear the value and save the lead.

---

## Where Dates Appear

Follow-up dates are visible in **Lead Detail** and available when editing a lead from the **Leads** list. Use them as a lightweight way to keep post-event action organized.
