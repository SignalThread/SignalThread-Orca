# Lead Star Rating

Star ratings give your team a quick read on lead quality. Use them to capture human judgment - fit, value, or relationship strength - while the conversation is still fresh.

---

## Rate a Lead

1. Open a lead from **Leads** or **Priority**.
2. Tap the star that matches your rating.
3. The rating saves automatically.

Tap the selected star again to clear the rating.

---

## Where Ratings Appear

Ratings are visible across the lead workflow:

- **Lead Detail** - on the lead card
- **Leads** - on each rated lead row
- **Priority** - alongside tier and name
- **Edit modal** - from the Leads list long-press action

---

## Rating vs. Temperature

Use stars and temperature together:

- **Stars** capture lead quality or strategic value
- **Temperature** captures urgency and follow-up priority

A lead can be high-value but not urgent, or urgent even if the fit is still developing.

See [Lead Temperature](lead-temperature.md).
