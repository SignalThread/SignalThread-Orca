# Delete a Lead

Delete a lead only when the record should be permanently removed from your account. Deletion removes the contact record, notes, recordings, and AI output tied to that lead.

---

## Before You Delete

Use deletion for duplicate records, test captures, or contacts that should not remain in your event pipeline. If the lead may still be useful for reporting or team follow-up, update its rating or temperature instead.

---

## Delete from Lead Detail

1. Open the lead from **Leads**.
2. Tap **Delete** in the top-right corner.
3. Review the confirmation message.
4. Tap **Delete** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep the record.

---

## What Is Removed

Deleting a lead removes:

- Contact information
- Star rating and temperature
- Follow-up date
- Voice notes and recordings
- AI Insights and AI Brief content connected to the lead

This action cannot be undone.

---

## Delete from the Leads List

You can also delete without opening the record:

1. Go to **Leads**.
2. Long-press the lead.
3. Select **Delete**.
4. Confirm the deletion.

---

## Common Issues

**A lead was deleted by mistake.**
Deleted records cannot be restored. If the contact is still available at the event, capture the lead again.

**The Delete button is not visible.**
Return to the top of the **Lead Detail** screen and confirm you are viewing the lead record, not a modal or note view.
