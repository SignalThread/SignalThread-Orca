# AI Insights Not Ready

AI Insights are generated after recordings and voice notes are uploaded, transcribed, and analyzed. If insights are not ready yet, the lead is still usable - the system is simply finishing the background work.

---

## How Insights Are Created

The workflow has a few stages:

1. **Recording** - audio is saved on your device
2. **Upload** - audio is sent for processing
3. **Transcription** - speech is converted to text
4. **Insight generation** - the transcript is analyzed for needs, objections, sentiment, and next steps

Progress appears in the **Context & Notes** card on the lead Overview tab.

---

## Status Labels

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Uploading... | Audio is being sent for processing |
| Processing transcript... | The transcript is being generated |
| Transcript ready | Text is available; insights may still be updating |
| Insights ready | AI Insights are available in the Overview tab |
| Uploading when connection improves... | The device is offline and upload is queued |
| Processing failed | Transcription or insight generation needs attention |

See [Voice Note Status Labels](../voice-notes/voice-note-status-labels.md).

---

## If Insights Are Taking Longer Than Expected

**Check connection quality.**
Weak venue Wi-Fi can delay uploads. Offline notes remain queued until connection improves.

**Give processing a few minutes.**
Longer recordings take more time. Pull down to refresh the lead when you return to the record.

**Review individual notes.**
Tap **View all notes** on the Context & Notes card to see which recording is still processing.

---

## If Processing Failed

Occasional processing failures can happen. If a note fails:

1. Review whether the audio was recorded clearly.
2. Add a short context voice note summarizing the key points.
3. Contact support if failures continue.

---

## AI Insights vs. AI Brief

- **AI Insights** are generated from your recordings and voice notes for a specific lead.
- **AI Brief** is generated from available public and event data before or during the conversation.

See [AI Brief](ai-brief.md).
