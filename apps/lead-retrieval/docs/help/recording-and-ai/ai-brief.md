# AI Brief

AI Brief gives your team pre-meeting context before the conversation starts. It is designed to help reps walk into booth conversations with sharper questions, stronger context, and a clearer reason to engage.

---

## What Is Included

When available, an AI Brief can include:

- A concise person and company snapshot
- Why the lead may be relevant at this event
- Suggested talking points
- Questions to ask
- Competitive or market context
- Cues to listen for during the conversation

Use the brief as preparation, not as a script. The best conversations still come from listening well and adding your own context.

---

## Open the AI Brief

1. Open a lead.
2. Tap the **AI Brief** tab.
3. Review the briefing before or during the conversation.

If the tab is unavailable, the brief is not ready yet or there is not enough source data.

---

## When Briefs Are Ready

AI Briefs are generated from available public and event data. Some leads have rich context quickly; others may have limited information.

If a brief is missing, continue using the lead record. Ratings, temperature, recordings, and voice notes still help your team qualify the opportunity.

In the Admin Portal, approved Pre-Sales Briefs may also appear on lead records when the brief workflow is enabled. Admin Portal briefs are prepared from captured lead context and should be reviewed before outreach.

---

## Common Issues

**The AI Brief tab is disabled.**
The brief is still generating or source data is limited. Pull down to refresh after a short wait.

**The brief looks light.**
Some leads have limited public information. Add conversation notes so AI Insights can capture what you learn directly.
