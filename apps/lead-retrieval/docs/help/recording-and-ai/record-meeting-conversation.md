# Record a Meeting or Conversation

Recordings turn live conversations into durable sales context. Capture what was said, then let transcripts and AI Insights help the team understand needs, objections, buying signals, and next steps.

---

## Before You Record

Confirm microphone permission is enabled and make sure the lead record is open. Recording from the right lead keeps the conversation attached to the right contact and event.

See [Camera & Microphone Permissions](../permissions/camera-microphone-permissions.md).

---

## Start Recording Manually

1. Open the lead.
2. Tap the microphone control.
3. Confirm the recording indicator is visible.
4. Continue the conversation naturally.

Use manual recording when you want control over exactly which parts of the conversation are captured.

### Automatic Recording

If **Auto-record meetings** is enabled, recording begins when a captured lead opens. This is useful for high-volume events where reps need to move quickly.

See [Auto-Record Meetings](../settings-and-account/auto-record-meetings.md).

---

## Stop Recording

Tap the stop control when the conversation ends. If you leave the lead record, recording may stop automatically depending on the current workflow.

After stopping, the note begins uploading and processing.

---

## What Happens After Recording

The recording moves through upload, transcription, and AI processing. Once ready, the transcript and AI Insights become available from the lead record.

If you remember something important after the conversation, add a context voice note so the insight history stays complete.

---

## Tips for Better Recordings

- Keep the phone close enough to capture both sides of the conversation.
- Move away from loud speakers or crowded aisles when possible.
- Add a quick voice note after the meeting for details that were implied but not said clearly.

---

## Common Issues

**Recording does not start.**
Microphone permission may be blocked. Check device settings.

**The transcript is delayed.**
Processing depends on upload speed and recording length. See [AI Insights Not Ready](ai-insights-not-ready.md).

**The conversation happened offline.**
The recording is saved locally and uploads when connection returns.
