# Priority Screen

Priority helps your team focus follow-up on the leads most likely to matter. It brings high-value and high-urgency leads into one view so the next conversation or outreach step is easier to choose.

---

## What Appears in Priority

Priority can include leads with strong scores, Hot temperature, high ratings, or signals that suggest timely follow-up. It is designed for quick triage rather than full pipeline review.

Use the Leads list when you need the complete event record.

---

## Read the Priority List

Leads are grouped by importance so you can scan quickly:

- Lead name and company
- Temperature
- Star rating
- Follow-up date, when available
- Event or company context, depending on your scope

Tap a lead to open **Lead Detail** and review the full record.

---

## Event vs. Company Scope

If your account has access to multiple events, Priority may show either the active event or broader company activity, depending on the current view. Confirm the active event in Settings when you are working from the event floor.

See [Active Event](../settings-and-account/active-event.md).

---

## Keep Priority Useful

Priority becomes more valuable when the team consistently adds:

- Star ratings
- Lead temperature
- Follow-up dates
- Voice notes with qualification context

These signals help the best leads rise to the top.

---

## Empty State

If Priority is empty, no leads currently meet the priority criteria. Capture more leads, add ratings, or update temperature to build the queue.
