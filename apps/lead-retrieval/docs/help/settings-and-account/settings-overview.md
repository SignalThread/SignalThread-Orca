# Settings Overview

Settings is the operational hub for the mobile app. Use it to confirm the active event, manage capture preferences, adjust account details, and keep event-floor workflows ready.

---

## Sections at a Glance

### Active Event
Choose where new leads, scans, and recordings are saved.
Read more: [Active Event](active-event.md)

### Capture Settings
Review or reset the learned badge layout for the active event.
Read more: [Badge Layout](badge-layout.md)

### Account
Edit your profile, sign out, or manage account-level actions.
Read more: [Edit Profile](edit-profile.md) · [Sign Out & Delete Account](sign-out-delete-account.md)

### Appearance
Choose the app theme that fits your working environment.
Read more: [Change App Theme](change-app-theme.md)

### Preferences
Turn **Auto-record meetings** on or off for future captures.
Read more: [Auto-Record Meetings](auto-record-meetings.md)

### Event Activity
Review a lightweight snapshot of lead activity for the active event.

---

## Offline Banner

When connection is unavailable, Settings shows:

> **Offline mode** - Showing last saved event and profile

You can keep capturing. Event and account data refresh when connection returns.
