# Sign Out & Delete Account

Use account controls carefully. Signing out only removes the session from your device; deleting an account permanently removes access and associated user data.

---

## Sign Out

Signing out is useful when switching devices, handing a shared device back to an admin, or ending a temporary session. Your synced leads remain available when you sign back in.

### How to Sign Out

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Find **Account**.
3. Tap **Sign Out**.
4. Wait for the app to return to sign in.

---

## Delete Your Account

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile and access. This cannot be undone.

Before deleting, make sure any data your organization needs has been exported or reviewed by an admin.

### How to Delete

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Open **Edit Profile**.
3. Select **Delete Account**.
4. Read the confirmation.
5. Complete the required acknowledgment and password step.
6. Tap **Delete Account**.

### What Gets Deleted

- Your user profile and display name
- Access to assigned events
- Leads, voice notes, recordings, and AI data tied to your account

Organization-level records may remain available to other authorized team members, depending on your account configuration.

---

## Common Issues

**You cannot sign back in.**
Check your credentials or use password reset from the sign-in screen.

**You deleted an account by mistake.**
Contact support immediately. Recovery is not guaranteed.
