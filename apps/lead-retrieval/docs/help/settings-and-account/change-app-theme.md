# Change App Theme

Theme settings help the app stay readable in different event environments, from bright expo halls to dim reception spaces.

---

## Change Theme

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Find **Appearance**.
3. Select **Light**, **Dark**, or **System**.

The change applies immediately.

---

## Theme Options

| Option | Best for |
|--------|----------|
| **Light** | Bright rooms, outdoor spaces, or daytime use |
| **Dark** | Dim venues, evening receptions, or lower-light environments |
| **System** | Matching your device setting automatically |

---

## Recommendation

Use **System** if your device already adapts to your environment. Choose **Light** or **Dark** when you want a consistent app appearance during an event.
