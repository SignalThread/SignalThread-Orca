# Edit Your Profile

Your profile controls how your name appears across the app. Keep it current so captured activity, team context, and account details are easy to recognize.

---

## Update Your Name

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Find **Account**.
3. Tap **Edit Profile**.
4. Update **Full name**.
5. Tap **Save**.

The updated name appears throughout the app after saving.

---

## What Can Be Changed

The profile screen currently supports editing your display name. Email address and access are managed through your organization account.

---

## Common Issues

**The name did not update.**
Confirm you tapped **Save** and have an active connection.

**The email address needs to change.**
Contact your organization's admin or support.
