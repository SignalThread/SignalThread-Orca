# Auto-Record Meetings

Auto-record helps busy event teams capture conversation context without adding another step to the booth workflow. When enabled, recording starts automatically after a scanned lead opens.

---

## Turn Auto-Record On or Off

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Find **Preferences**.
3. Toggle **Auto-record meetings**.

The setting takes effect immediately for future captures.

---

## How It Works

When auto-record is on:

1. You scan a badge or capture a lead.
2. The lead opens in **Lead Detail**.
3. Recording starts automatically.
4. The audio is saved to the lead and processed for transcripts and AI Insights.

Use this when every booth conversation should be captured by default.

---

## When to Use It

Auto-record is useful for:

- High-volume events where reps need fewer manual steps
- Teams that want consistent AI Insights across conversations
- Booth workflows where every scan usually leads to a live conversation

Turn it off when your team scans many contacts without holding a conversation.

---

## Permission Required

Auto-record needs microphone access. If recording does not start, check permissions.

See [Camera & Microphone Permissions](../permissions/camera-microphone-permissions.md).

---

## Manual Recording Still Works

Even with auto-record off, you can start recording manually from **Lead Detail**.
