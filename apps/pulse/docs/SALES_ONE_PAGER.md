# Sales One-Pager

Detailed product marketing and sales-enablement copy grounded in current implementation.

## Product Summary

SignalThread is a voice-first feedback platform for location-based operators and event teams. It captures spoken customer feedback at the point of experience, processes that audio with AI, and converts it into operational signals teams can act on quickly.

The platform combines a premium kiosk flow, automated transcription and analysis, and account-level operations for multi-location deployment.

## The Problem We Solve

Most feedback programs create lag:

- responses are unstructured
- teams manually read/listen to data
- insight quality varies by reviewer%
- actions are delayed or unclear

SignalThread compresses that workflow into one system: collect voice, process automatically, prioritize clearly, and drill into supporting evidence.

## How the Platform Works

1. A customer interacts with a branded kiosk survey and records spoken answers.
2. Audio is uploaded securely to object storage through a verified upload pipeline.
3. AI transcription converts speech to text.
4. AI analysis generates summary and sentiment outputs.
5. Dashboard signals highlight overall health, trend direction, friction drivers, and opportunity areas.
6. Teams drill into linked answer-level evidence to validate decisions.
