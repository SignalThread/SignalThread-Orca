This guide walks through the first setup steps for using SignalThread to collect voice feedback.

## What You Need

- An email address you can access
- A modern browser
- A device with microphone access for kiosk use

Recommended browsers for kiosk capture:

- Chrome
- Safari

## Sign In

1. Go to the login page.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Click `Send code`.
4. Enter the 6-digit code sent to your email.

If you get stuck, the login page includes a support contact:

- `support@signalthread.ai`

## First-Time Setup Flow

After you sign in, the app is organized around a few main areas:

- `Dashboard`: your main survey and analytics view
- `Create Survey`: where you build a new survey
- `Profile Settings`: where you manage locations/teams, branding, consent, billing, and users
- `Help`: the in-app help page with walkthrough and FAQ

## Recommended Setup Order

1. Add or confirm your `Location/Team`
2. Create your first survey
3. Choose a question voice
4. Save the survey
5. Launch the kiosk
6. Review responses and insights

## What “Location/Team” Means

A `Location/Team` is where feedback is collected.

Examples:

- a coffee shop
- a retail store
- an office
- a field crew
- a landscaping team

What this means:

- You do not need a physical storefront to use the app.
- Teams and mobile crews can use the same setup pattern as fixed locations.

## Before You Launch a Live Survey

Before you send people into the kiosk flow, make sure:

- Your survey has at least one question
- The right location/team is attached
- Your consent screen looks correct
- Branding is set the way you want
- Voice preview sounds right
- You have tested the kiosk link on the device you plan to use

## If You See Empty Screens

The app may show an onboarding-style empty state if you do not yet have a real survey. In that case:

1. Go to `Create Survey`
2. Build your questions
3. Save the survey
4. Return to the dashboard
