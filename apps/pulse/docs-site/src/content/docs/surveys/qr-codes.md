Use QR codes to open a live survey kiosk quickly on another device.

## Where QR Codes Appear

QR access is available from survey surfaces in the app, including:

- the main dashboard survey rows
- analytics/detail screens for a survey

## How QR Works Now

The QR preview is no longer treated like a raw image link.

Instead, the app shows a dedicated QR interface with explicit actions:

- `View QR`
- `Download PNG`
- `Copy Link`

## Open the QR Modal

1. Find the survey
2. Open the QR action
3. Click `View QR` or the QR preview trigger

The app opens an in-app modal that shows:

- a larger QR code
- the survey name
- the public kiosk URL
- `Download PNG`
- `Copy Link`

## Download the QR Code

1. Open the QR modal
2. Click `Download PNG`

The app exports a higher-resolution PNG with padding around the code.

What this means:

- the PNG is better suited for printing
- the code should scan more reliably
- users do not need to right-click or use a browser image menu

## Copy the Kiosk Link

1. Open the QR modal
2. Click `Copy Link`

You can share that copied URL directly in:

- text messages
- email
- internal team chat

## What the QR Code Opens

The QR code points to the survey kiosk flow for that specific survey.

The destination URL stays the same even though the visual QR experience was improved.

## Tips for Better Scanning

- keep some white space around the printed code
- avoid stretching or compressing the image
- test the QR code on the actual device you plan to use
- if printing, use the downloaded PNG instead of a screenshot
