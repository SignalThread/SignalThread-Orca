# Prepare for the Final 30 Days

Shift from broad planning into deadline, readiness, and exception management.

## Objective

Convert broad planning into exception-only review across deadlines, schedule, money, speakers, documents, and communications.

## Orca Modules

- Roadmap
- Action Center
- Run of Show
- Budget
- Speakers
- Docs

## Before You Begin

- Confirm the event dates, timezone, and organization context.
- Gather the current source files and identify the planner responsible for each planning area.

## Workflow

1. Filter Roadmap to incomplete, at-risk, critical, and blocked work due before the event.
2. Review Action Center approvals and deadlines across the portfolio.
3. Freeze the working schedule cadence and review every Run of Show conflict.
4. Resolve budget approvals and identify remaining forecast exposure.
5. Close speaker profile, deck, travel, dietary, and document gaps.
6. Confirm current production documents are approved and easy to find in Docs.
7. Move the weekly review to a shorter exception-focused cadence as the event approaches.

## What Complete Means

- Critical and overdue Roadmap work is owned and dated.
- Run of Show conflicts, budget approvals, speaker gaps, and document reviews are in exception-only lists.
- The team knows which items remain outside Orca and who owns them.

## Planner Tips

- Increase review frequency as the event approaches without duplicating the source records.
- Freeze external communications only after checking the underlying audience, schedule, and speaker data.
