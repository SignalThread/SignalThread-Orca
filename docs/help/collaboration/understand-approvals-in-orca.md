# Understand Approvals in Orca

Understand how approval decisions belong to Budget, Docs, Marketing, and speaker submission records.

## Overview

Approvals are not one universal object. Budget submissions, document reviews, marketing sends, and speaker submissions each have their own states and actions.

Changing a Roadmap or task status does not approve a source record.

## When You’ll Use It

- Open this guide when you need to understand how approval decisions belong to Budget, Docs, Marketing, and speaker submission records.
- Return when a notification, approval, or availability question needs to be traced to its source record.

## Before You Begin

- Confirm the organization and event before following a linked item.
- Use the source module's access and status rules for any change or decision.

## Open Approvals in Orca

1. Open **Action Center** > **Approvals** to find cross-event approval work.
2. Open the linked Budget, Docs Hub, Marketing, or speaker record to act on it.

## Find and Complete an Approval

1. Open Action Center and choose Approvals, or open the owning module.
2. Open the source record and review its current content and history.
3. Choose the approval action available for that record type.
4. Confirm the resulting state in the source module.

## Understand Important Concepts

- Approvals are not one universal object. Budget submissions, document reviews, marketing sends, and speaker submissions each have their own states and actions.
- Changing a Roadmap or task status does not approve a source record.

## In-product Helper Text

> Complete approvals in the source module linked from Action Center.

## Planner Tips

- Keep decision context on the source record so a later planner can understand what was approved and why.

## Best Practices

- Make the decision in the source record so its history remains complete.
- Use Action Center to find approvals, not as a replacement approval system.

## Troubleshooting

### A control is missing or disabled

Open the linked source module and check its record state; summary surfaces do not override source permissions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is the system of record?

The linked source module owns the record. Notifications, Action Center, and disabled Tasks do not replace module approvals.
