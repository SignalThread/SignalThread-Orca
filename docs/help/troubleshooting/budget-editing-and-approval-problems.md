# Budget Editing and Approval Problems

Resolve read-only lines, failed submissions, missing reviewers, and unexpected approval state.

## Start Here

1. Confirm you have edit access to the event.
2. Check whether the line is approved, locked, pending, rejected, or in revision.
3. Open the line's approval history before changing its values.
4. Provide a reviewer and required message when submitting.
5. Pull back your own pending submission when the current workflow allows revision.
6. Complete approval in Budget or through the Action Center link; changing another status does not approve the line.

## If the Problem Continues

Record the event name, affected record, action you attempted, visible status, and exact error text. Share that context with the appropriate Orca administrator without including secure portal or intake tokens.

## Best Practices

- Open approval history before changing or resubmitting a line.
- Pull back eligible submissions instead of duplicating a locked or pending line.
