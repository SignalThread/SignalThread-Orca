# Submit and Review Budget Approvals

Route budget lines for a reviewer decision and maintain a clear approval history.

## Overview

Approval is attached to the budget record. A task status or message does not approve the line.

Revision and submission history preserve the decision trail.

## When You’ll Use It

- Open this guide when you need to route budget lines for a reviewer decision and maintain a clear approval history.
- Return during early planning, weekly review, client review, final preparation, and closeout whenever this record changes.

## Before You Begin

- Open the correct event and confirm its name and dates.
- Editing requires event editor access; Viewers can inspect supported pages but cannot save protected changes.

## Open Submit and Review Budget Approvals

1. Open **Budget** > **Full Budget Grid**.
2. Select a line's **Needs submission** status, or select **View approvals** to open **Budget Approvals**.

## Submit a Line for Approval

1. Open the line item in the Full Budget Grid.
2. Select **Submit for approval**.
3. Select the available reviewer or recipients and add a useful review message.
4. Submit and confirm the line shows a pending approval state.

## Review a Submission

1. Open the pending approval from Budget or Action Center.
2. Compare the requested line details with supporting context.
3. Select **Approve** or **Reject** and provide context when requested.
4. Use Pull back when you submitted the item and need to revise it before a decision.

## Understand Important Concepts

- Approval is attached to the budget record. A task status or message does not approve the line.
- Revision and submission history preserve the decision trail.

## In-product Helper Text

> Submit for approval only when the line is ready for a reviewer decision.

## Planner Tips

- Write the decision needed in the reviewer message: amount, vendor, scope, and deadline.

## Best Practices

- Include the amount, vendor, scope, and required decision date in the optional message.
- Pull back a submission before editing it; do not create a duplicate line to bypass review history.

## Troubleshooting

### A control is missing or disabled

Check your event role, the record's current approval or lock state, and whether the documented feature is available in production.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is the system of record?

The budget line and its **Budget Approvals** history are authoritative. Action Center only brings eligible approval work into a cross-event queue.
