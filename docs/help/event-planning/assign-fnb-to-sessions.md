# Assign F&B to Sessions

Connect approved catalog items or F&B requirements to the sessions where service occurs.

## Overview

A catalog assignment ties a reusable approved item to a session. A freeform F&B requirement may be used when no approved catalog item fits.

Supported assignments can connect to budget requirements or line items.

## When You’ll Use It

- Open this guide when you need to connect approved catalog items or F&B requirements to the sessions where service occurs.
- Return during early planning, weekly review, client review, final preparation, and closeout whenever this record changes.

## Before You Begin

- Open the correct event and confirm its name and dates.
- Editing requires event editor access; Viewers can inspect supported pages but cannot save protected changes.

## Open Assign F&B to Sessions

1. Open **Run of Show**, select a session, and open **F&B** in the quick drawer or session workspace.
2. Search existing approved catalog items, add an item, then select **Save changes** in the quick drawer when prompted.

## Assign a Catalog Item

1. Confirm the item appears in Approved Catalog Items in the F&B Catalog.
2. Open Run of Show and select the session.
3. Open the F&B quick module or session workspace section.
4. Add the approved item and enter supported service details.
5. Save and verify the assignment on the session.

## Understand Important Concepts

- A catalog assignment ties a reusable approved item to a session. A freeform F&B requirement may be used when no approved catalog item fits.
- Supported assignments can connect to budget requirements or line items.

## In-product Helper Text

> Choose approved catalog items for consistent names and pricing across sessions.

## Planner Tips

- Assign meals to the actual service session, including staff or rehearsal meals, so timing and budget review stay aligned.

## Best Practices

- Assign existing approved items to the service session where they are consumed.
- When an assignment is budget-linked, review the linked Budget line before changing quantity, price, tax, or service charge.

## Troubleshooting

### A control is missing or disabled

Check your event role, the record's current approval or lock state, and whether the documented feature is available in production.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is the system of record?

The session's **Session F&B Plan** owns its item assignments and service estimate. **Budget** owns linked financial approval and actual or committed amounts.
