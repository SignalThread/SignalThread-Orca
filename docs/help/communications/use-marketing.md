# Use Marketing

Plan event email campaigns, audiences, sends, approvals, suppressions, and delivery outcomes.

## Overview

Email is the supported campaign channel. Other channels are labeled as later work.

Marketing is available under **Management** in the event sidebar.

## When You’ll Use It

- Open this guide when you need to plan event email campaigns, audiences, sends, approvals, suppressions, and delivery outcomes.
- Return before contacting an audience and before reporting send or compliance outcomes.

## Before You Begin

- Open the correct event and clean the relevant Directory identities before building an audience.
- Confirm you have event editor access and the authority to communicate with the selected recipients.

## Open Marketing

1. Open an event and select **Marketing** under **Management**.
2. Use **Overview**, **Campaigns**, **Calendar**, **Performance**, and **Compliance**.

## Review Marketing Work

1. Select **Marketing** under **Management** in the event sidebar.
2. Review campaigns, email sends, performance, audiences, and suppressions.
3. Open a campaign or send for details and available actions.
4. Use status, owner, dates, and outcome data to identify follow-up.

## Understand Important Concepts

- Email is the supported campaign channel. Other channels are labeled as later work.
- Marketing is available under **Management** in the event sidebar.

## In-product Helper Text

> Marketing manages event email work; Directory supplies canonical people and can create audiences.

## Planner Tips

- Separate campaign intent from individual sends: one campaign can organize several invitations, reminders, and follow-ups.

## Best Practices

- Use Campaigns for the initiative and Performance for individual send outcomes.
- Build audiences from clean Directory identities and review Compliance before external sends.

## Troubleshooting

### A control is missing or disabled

Check the send or campaign state, required audience and sender fields, approval state, and your event access.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is the system of record?

Marketing owns campaigns, audience snapshots, sends, approvals, suppressions, and delivery outcomes; Directory owns the underlying person identity.
