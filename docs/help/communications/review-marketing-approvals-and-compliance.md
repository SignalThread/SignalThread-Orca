# Review Marketing Approvals and Compliance

Review email content, recipients, approval state, suppressions, and unsubscribe behavior before sending.

## Overview

Rejected approval cancels the email send in the current workflow.

Orca appends an unsubscribe footer to email previews and sends.

## When You’ll Use It

- Open this guide when you need to review email content, recipients, approval state, suppressions, and unsubscribe behavior before sending.
- Return before contacting an audience and before reporting send or compliance outcomes.

## Before You Begin

- Open the correct event and clean the relevant Directory identities before building an audience.
- Confirm you have event editor access and the authority to communicate with the selected recipients.

## Open Review Marketing Approvals and Compliance

1. Open **Marketing** and select **Performance** to review sends or **Compliance** to review suppressions.
2. Open an email send to review its approval state and available actions.

## Review a Send

1. Open the email send.
2. Review the audience snapshot, sender settings, subject, summary, content, and recipient preview.
3. Check approval state and respond with Approve, Reject, or Request changes when available.
4. Confirm suppressions and unsubscribe handling before scheduling or sending.

## Review Suppressions

1. Open the suppressions area.
2. Review email, reason, source, and creation date.
3. Use Resubscribe only when you have a valid operational basis and the available action applies.

## Understand Important Concepts

- Rejected approval cancels the email send in the current workflow.
- Orca appends an unsubscribe footer to email previews and sends.

## In-product Helper Text

> Approval covers this email send; it does not approve every send in the campaign.

## Planner Tips

- Have the approver verify the recipient definition, not only the copy. Audience mistakes are often more consequential than wording changes.

## Best Practices

- Approve the recipient definition and sender settings as carefully as the message copy.
- Use **Resubscribe** only when the event-specific suppression should legitimately be removed.

## Troubleshooting

### A control is missing or disabled

Check the send or campaign state, required audience and sender fields, approval state, and your event access.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is the system of record?

Marketing owns campaigns, audience snapshots, sends, approvals, suppressions, and delivery outcomes; Directory owns the underlying person identity.
